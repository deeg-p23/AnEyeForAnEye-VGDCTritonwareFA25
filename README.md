## Wicked Wagers
> "It's the busiest time of the year for witches, Halloween! For the sake of your wallet and your pride, outcompete your fellow witch's potion business." – Wicked Wager's Itch.io Description

Wicked Wagers is a local co-op competitive game developed in two weeks for VGDC @ UCSD's Tritonware Game Jam of Fall 2025. The game is a quick 5 minutes, where one player takes the left side of the keyboard, another player takes the right side,
and both keep mashing and timing button presses until one of them comes out on top. 

If you have a friend next to you, feel free to try it out for yourselves [here](https://deeg-p23.itch.io/wicked-wagers).
